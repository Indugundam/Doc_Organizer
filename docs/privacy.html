Doc Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Doc Manager is an app for organizing receipts, bills and other documents into folders on your phone. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your documents stay on your device and, only if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not run any servers and we never receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Folders and documents you create, scan or import are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on Google Drive sync or use the Share or Download options yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Connect Google Drive in Settings, you sign in with your Google account and the app backs up your folders to a folder named DocManager in your own Google Drive. Backup is one-way: the Drive folder mirrors your device, and the app never downloads or changes anything on your device from Drive. Documents you delete in the app are moved to your Google Drive trash.

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission. It can access only the files and folders it creates. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your documents are transferred directly between your phone and Google Drive. They do not pass through, and are not stored on, any server operated by us.
  • The app uses your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected in Settings.
  • Files in Google Drive are stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and count towards your own Google storage.

Doc Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and record videos.
  • Microphone: to record audio with videos.
  • Photos and videos: to import images and videos you pick, and to save them.
  • Internet: used only for Google sign-in and Google Drive sync.

Each permission is used only when you start that action.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not share your data with anyone.

Removing your data

  • Stop syncing: tap Disconnect in the app's Settings. Files remain on your phone and in your Drive.
  • Revoke access: remove Doc Manager at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete synced files: delete the DocManager folder from your Google Drive.
  • Delete local files: delete them in the app, or uninstall the app.

Children

Doc Manager is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy
