Doc Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Doc Manager is an app for organizing receipts, bills and other documents into folders on your phone. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your documents stay on your device and, only if you choose, in your own Google Drive. We do not run any servers and we never receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Folders and documents you create, scan or import are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on Google Drive sync or use the Share or Download options yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

Search inside documents

So you can search by what is written in a document, the app reads the text in your photos, scans and PDFs. This happens entirely on your device, using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The text it finds is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to Google Drive, to us, or anywhere else.

ML Kit may send Google anonymous statistics about how the text recognition feature performs, such as how long it took and whether it succeeded, and may download updates. These statistics never include your images, documents or the text found in them. See ML Kit Terms.

Reminders

If you set an expiry or renewal reminder on a document, the reminder's date and type are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. Reminders are not backed up to Google Drive or sent anywhere else. Notifications are scheduled and shown by your phone's operating system; they include the document's name and the reminder date, so they may be visible on your lock screen depending on your phone's notification settings. Deleting a document removes its reminder.

App lock

If you turn on App lock in Settings, the app asks you to unlock it with your phone's fingerprint, face unlock or screen lock PIN, pattern or passcode. This check is done by your phone's operating system. The app never sees, receives or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN; the phone only tells it whether unlocking succeeded.

Hide in recent apps stops your phone from showing the app's contents in the recent-apps screen. On Android 12 and older it also blocks screenshots inside the app while it is turned on.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Connect Google Drive in Settings, you sign in with your Google account and the app backs up your folders to a folder named DocManager in your own Google Drive. Backup is one-way: the Drive folder mirrors your device, and the app never downloads or changes anything on your device from Drive. Documents you delete in the app are moved to your Google Drive trash.

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission. It can access only the files and folders it creates. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your documents are transferred directly between your phone and Google Drive. They do not pass through, and are not stored on, any server operated by us.
  • The app uses your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected in Settings.
  • The app reads the size of its backup folder and your Google account's overall storage usage and limit, only to show them in Settings. These figures are kept on your phone and not shared.
  • Files in Google Drive are stored by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and count towards your own Google storage.

Doc Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and record videos.
  • Microphone: to record audio with videos.
  • Photos and videos: to import images and videos you pick, and to save them.
  • Notifications: to show the document reminders you set.
  • Run at startup: only to re-schedule your reminders after the phone restarts or the app is updated.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint / face unlock): only to unlock the app when App lock is on.
  • Internet: used for Google sign-in and Google Drive backup, and by ML Kit as described above.

Each permission is used only when you start that action or turn on that setting.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of our own. (The only statistics sent anywhere are ML Kit's anonymous performance statistics described above.)
  • We do not share your data with anyone.

Removing your data

  • Stop syncing: tap Disconnect in the app's Settings. Files remain on your phone and in your Drive.
  • Revoke access: remove Doc Manager at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete synced files: delete the DocManager folder from your Google Drive.
  • Delete local files: delete them in the app, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also deletes the text read from your documents for search and any reminders you set.

Children

Doc Manager is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
