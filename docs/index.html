Doc Manager

Keep receipts, bills and documents organized in folders on your phone.

Features

  • Create folders and subfolders, for example "Bills" or "Hospital".
  • Scan documents with the camera into PDFs, or import photos, videos and files.
  • Search, rename, share and download your documents.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: connect your Google account in Settings to back up your folders to a DocManager folder in your own Google Drive.

Your data

Documents are stored on your device. If you turn on Google Drive sync, they are also stored in your own Google Drive, and the app can access only the files it creates there. We run no servers and never receive your documents. See the Privacy Policy for details.
